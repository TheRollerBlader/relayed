Same requests. Same stock. First-come is always labelled heuristic. Suitability on this pair is exact. Request 2 is unmatched under first-come because capacity for that slot is exhausted.

Same two charging requests. Request 1 can take either slot. Request 2 needs the accessible slot. There is one of each. First-come gives the accessible charger to request 1 and leaves request 2 unmatched. That method is labelled heuristic. Suitability gives the accessible slot to request 2 and the general slot to request 1. Both match. On this size, that method is exact. These are worked examples from the matcher, not a headcount from a live hall.

That what-if does not change the real resource list. Approving a stale plan reserves nothing. Longest-wait adds one weight every thirty minutes. We call that a declared rule.
